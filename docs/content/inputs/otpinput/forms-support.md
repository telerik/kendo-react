# Forms Support

The KendoReact OTPInput component can be easily integrated with our [KendoReact Form]({% slug overview_form %}). The form enables you to validate the OTPInput and prevent the submission of the forms which are in an invalid state.

By utilizing the KendoReact Form component we can take advantage of the [Validation]({% slug validation_form %}) functionality and render additional custom component depending on the form's validity state.
